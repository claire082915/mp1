Shelp is a smart virtual pantry management app designed to help users reduce food waste and save money by allowing users to manually log food items alongside their expiration dates, which are organized chronologically from nearest to latest expiry. Near the expiration date, the user will receive a notification to their phone. The app also leverages AI to suggest custom recipe ideas based on near-expiry ingredients selected by the user.
